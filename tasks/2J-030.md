## Delivered

- Weekly calendar uses `Atlantic/Canary`; recurring templates and dated sessions stay separate. Occurrences require an explicit date, and active templates can be explicitly archived with an audit record.
- Sessions use active 2J services and areas. Staff can create group sessions or an individual appointment bound to one `client_id`; its client identity cannot silently change, and capacity is fixed at one.
- Staff reservations, reservation cancellation, attendance/no-show, and waitlist entry/withdrawal work against PostgreSQL transactions. A session row lock serializes seat allocation and session cancellation.
- Centre cancellation moves active reservations and waiting entries to `cancelled_by_center`, preserves their rows and audit history, prevents subsequent attendance/cancel actions, and cannot be reversed.
- Services and areas cannot be archived while they have future scheduled sessions or active future templates. Reservation and waitlist transactions revalidate service/area availability.
- Server permissions: Trainer and Admin can create/cancel sessions and templates, manage reservations/waitlist, correct attendance and authorize over-capacity. Grants remain separate as `reservations.manage`, `reservations.attendance` and `scheduling.override_capacity`; Reception/Management lacks attendance and override. PII checks remain server-side.
- Every attendance/no-show change records old/new state, actor and database time; every over-capacity write is audited and visibly marked.
- Premium responsive UI includes 2J confirmations for reservation/session cancellation and over-capacity approval, inline feedback, no full-week loading flicker, focus restoration, accessible keyboard-operable client search, visible individual appointments via a non-PII weekly `isIndividual` marker, human errors, `1 libre`/`N libres` copy and an organized 390 px reservation-action layout. Reopen is offered only for `attended` and `no_show`.
- The default eligibility resolver fails closed. Tests inject a server-owned eligible result; this task does not invent entitlement or consume balance.

## Explicitly excluded

Automatic waitlist promotion, penalties/cancellation windows, recurrence horizon/automation, push reminders, resources/rooms, full payment and balance consumption.
