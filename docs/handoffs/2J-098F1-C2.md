## Authoritative payment facts and access derivation

`economic_transactions` is the only payment/refund source. Validated payment transactions contribute their `amount_cents`; refund transactions subtract their amount. A term charge is settled when the resulting net is at least `economic_charges.amount_cents`. Pending transfer evidence is not a payment transaction and does not settle the charge. The evaluator re-reads this net for every eligibility decision, so a later refund can make an overdue assignment unavailable again.

The evaluator reads `assignment_contract_terms.deadline_computation` and `economic_charges.due_on`; it checks that the snapshot rule/due date, effective local date, charge/assignment/client/plan references, amount and term facts agree. Inconsistent or missing term facts fail closed as `entitlement_unavailable`. Exempt charges allow access. Void or unsupported term assignments fail closed. `payment_block='off'` permits booking while preserving the unpaid economic state.

For enforced terms, `due_on` remains inclusive. Blocking starts when the Atlantic/Canary business date reaches the persisted `paymentBlockEffectiveOn` date (the local midnight following `due_on`). Natural-month day 10 and grace-based +5-day boundaries therefore do not depend on server timezone or adding 24 hours. No mutable blocked/paid flag or `block_applied` event is authoritative.

The evaluator share-locks affected economic charge rows in stable id order, then reads the net in a separate statement. Payment and refund writers already lock the same charge `FOR UPDATE`. Under PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED, a booking waits for a concurrent economic writer or the writer waits for the booking transaction; the booking insert/consumption commits with the state it observed. This is a per-charge row lock, not a global or advisory lock.

## Legacy, booking, waitlist, and compatibility

- Assignments without `assignment_contract_terms` retain the existing 098E `payment_validated`, provisional-period and recurrence behavior.
- Term assignments use derived ledger/deadline state and bypass only the legacy per-period payment-status calculation. Their session quota still selects the single entitlement period containing the session's Atlantic/Canary date; monthly bags remain independent. Unlimited periods need no grant and report no numeric balance.
- Internal states distinguish `payment_pending` and `payment_overdue`. For API compatibility, an overdue assignment denies with the existing `debt_restriction` reason.
- An overdue assignment is removed from eligible alternatives; another independent assignment can remain eligible. The old global overdue-debt helper excludes charges linked to contract terms, so it cannot re-block unrelated rights. Legacy charges keep the existing global debt behavior.
- Existing confirmed reservations and their ledger consumption are not changed when a charge becomes overdue or is refunded.
- Waitlist join continues to ignore payment debt. Promotion uses normal eligibility and leaves an ineligible entry waiting with the current skipped-promotion audit reason.
- Existing staff debt override cannot bypass a term payment denial; the approved auditable assignment-override model remains a later F1-C block.
