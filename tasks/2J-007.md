# 2J-007 — Identity, permissions, and audit architecture

Priority: P0 · Risk: critical · Status: REVIEW

## Objective
Define server-enforced role and permission boundaries, session revocation contract, and append-only audit envelope before identity/runtime implementation.

## Scope and gates
- Use approved role names only: Admin, Entrenador, Recepción/Gestión, Contable.
- Machine-readable role starter matrix, deny-by-default evaluator, session-version validation, and audit JSON Schema/runtime guard with synthetic tests.
- Accountant exact scopes, health field-level access, role administration, identity provider/transport and retention remain explicit decisions; no new grants inferred.
- No login, customer accounts, production provider, migrations, credentials, legal retention choice, or production app change.

## Acceptance
- Server permission check denies unknown operations; explicit denies override grants; approved roles are tested across resources.
- Session expiration and monotonically versioned revocation are deterministic.
- Audit envelope requires actor/resource/action/correlation/time/outcome, is append-only by interface, rejects sensitive payload keys, and excludes unknown fields.
- Typecheck/tests/build/audit plus independent review and QA pass; critical change is never auto-merged.
