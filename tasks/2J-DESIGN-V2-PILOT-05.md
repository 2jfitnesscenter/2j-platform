# 2J-DESIGN-V2-PILOT-05 — Design System V2 pilot: Tienda profesional

## Objective
Migrate the professional Store to Design System V2: visual product catalogue, inventory table, sales list, keeping every existing flow, endpoint and idempotency rule.

## Out of scope / stop conditions
No backend, RBAC, API, database or migration change. No product images or uploader (media backend not implemented), no invented low-stock thresholds, no Member store redesign, no checkout. Stop and report on any real functional bug.

## Acceptance
Tests, typecheck and build pass; verified in the local pilot; owner decides `VISUAL_APPROVED` and `FUNCTIONAL_APPROVED` (both `no` until then). Handoff: `docs/handoffs/2J-DESIGN-V2-PILOT-05.md`.
