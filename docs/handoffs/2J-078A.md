## Central person / multi-facet identity

- `personId` is a proposed conceptual identity root for one human; this TASK implements no table, provider, identity resolver or account link.
- `centralClientId` remains the client-facet id used by reservations, membership, payments and Client 360. It is not the person root.
- A human may independently have client, staff/professional and Training facets. `staff.auth_actor_id` is an auth subject, not a person id. A future Hub signs in once and shows member/staff/admin actions only from contextual server-side capabilities.
- Platform and Training permissions stay local. A linked identity does not copy roles or grant access across domains.
- Client contact, staff operational/profile fields and Training display/preferences retain separate owners. Archive/deactivate/delete/unlink are independent; no cascade deletion.
- Future linking requires fresh proof of Platform identity and the existing Training account. If the person mapping is unavailable, defer linking instead of mapping directly to `centralClientId`.
- Conceptual acceptance cases A–E cover a regular client, trainer who is not a client, trainer/client/Training combination, admin without Training, and Reception later acquiring a client facet; ambiguous proof must fail closed.
