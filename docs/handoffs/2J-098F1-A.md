## Implemented

- Versioned `plan_versions` metadata: `term_kind`, `term_months`, `max_span_days`, `payment_rule`, `payment_block`. Legacy rows remain `NULL`; new fields are included in plan serialization and validated for compatible combinations.
- Pure date-only domain rules: `calculateContractEnd`, strict date validation, natural-month validation, and inclusive custom-range validation. The approved 3/6/12-month anniversary and end-of-month behavior is mirrored by immutable SQL function `contract_end_v1`.
- Immutable one-to-one `assignment_contract_terms` foundation with a restricted assignment FK, coherent term/date checks, frozen plan-version and charge-deadline snapshot validation, custom maximum-span enforcement, and guards against update, delete, and truncate. Deadline snapshots retain the rule, assignment date, inputs, and resulting due date.
- Contract-kind plans are intentionally omitted from the current assignable-plan list and rejected by the current sale path. This keeps the new contract model feature-inactive until a later approved phase; legacy plan sale behavior remains available.

## Compatibility

The migration performs no backfill and does not rewrite plan versions, assignments, charges, reservations, entitlement ledgers, or 0033 period records. Existing legacy plans retain `term_kind = NULL`. The 0033 period-bag model, 098E recurrence behavior, and 2J-CAPABILITIES-V2 / Training suspension logic remain unchanged. No generalized period generation or payment/access gate was added.
