# 2J-097Y — Owner polish and connected booking pilot

## Objective
Apply the owner's concrete usability feedback and make the synthetic Member booking → professional roster → Member status journey testable against one local PostgreSQL database. Keep all changes on one branch and one Draft PR; do not merge. Owner visual acceptance remains pending.

## P0/P1 scope
- Increase professional content inset consistently in the global AppShell.
- Provide a connected Member pilot account backed by the owner-review seed and canonical Member session/booking API, with explicit, manual session selection and confirmation.
- Rework desktop Agenda into a time grid; use a selected-day timeline/list on tablet and mobile.
- Keep simultaneous sessions valid across different professionals and configured service areas; reject same-professional overlaps. Do not introduce area exclusivity without an existing domain rule.
- Make weekly recurring timetable and one-off sessions clearly separate.
- Replace opaque plan selection with real plan/service/right details, gated price visibility, and safer period copy. Do not implement new billing semantics.
- Make Store catalog-first, moving creation, stock, and sales workflows into deliberate actions while reusing current APIs and permissions.
- Add local Member URLs, login instructions, synthetic data and an owner connected-pilot checklist.

## Decisions and stop conditions
The existing plan version supports `recurring` with weekly/monthly/custom-period cadence and `session_pack` with period-day validity. Assignment persists `starts_on`, nullable `ends_on`, charge `due_on`, a payment ledger state, immutable snapshots, and assignment events. It does not separately model natural-month service periods, payment grace policy, non-payment suspension, nor server-authoritative expiry automation. Do not infer quarter/half-year/annual semantics from arbitrary day counts or put grace dates into `ends_on`/`due_on`. No migration is included in this TASK. The required domain/migration proposal and follow-up decision are recorded in the handoff.

Area model contains named active/inactive areas only; sessions must reference their service's configured area. No area exclusivity/capacity constraint exists. Keep areas descriptive and use the existing professional overlap guard. Do not add area lock semantics or schema.

- At desktop, Agenda has weekday columns, vertical time, compact sessions and visible simultaneous lanes. At tablet/mobile it does not force seven narrow columns.
- No new migration, no production/Training/2jfitnessapp touch, no 2J-098 Activity.
- Evidence documents actual runs only; owner acceptance is pending.
