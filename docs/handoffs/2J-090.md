## Implementation summary

Migration `0028_store_v1.sql` adds fixed-category products, variants, append-only inventory movements, internal sales, immutable sale lines, and audit events. Sale lines snapshot product/variant names, SKU, integer-cent unit price, quantity, and total. Inventory balance is derived from the movement ledger; sales lock variant/product records and calculate totals from current server-side prices in one transaction. UUID idempotency keys protect retries and reject a different payload. Concurrent submissions with the same key take a transaction-scoped PostgreSQL advisory lock before replay lookup, so the waiting request replays the committed sale before checking reduced stock. Voiding is one-way and restores stock with sale-reversal movements once.

Member routes expose active products with coarse stock availability and the authenticated Member's own purchase history, without a client/person selector. No active fee or booking entitlement is required. Member preview remains fixture-only and includes synthetic nutrition, merchandising, and purchase history.

Admin routes cover product/variant management, stock movements, sale creation/listing, and void. Store capabilities are registered server-side and only Admin has them in the current role map; Trainers, Reception/Management, and Accountants are denied until explicit authorization exists.
