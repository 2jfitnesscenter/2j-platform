## Implemented

- Reuses and generalizes the existing 0033 `assignment_entitlement_periods` and `client_entitlement_ledger` period references. No parallel period or balance model was added, and no historical period, assignment, or ledger row was rewritten.
- Adds the versioned `plan_rights.quantity_rule` values `weekday_ratio_v1`, `fixed_v1`, and `unlimited`. Legacy `sessions_quota` values remain NULL and retain their existing behavior; no backfill occurs.
- Adds date-only contract splitting at calendar month boundaries. Each quota subperiod is an independent bag. Weekdays are inclusive Monday–Friday dates, and the session formula is integer round-half-up: `floor((2 * weekdays * weekly_frequency + 5) / 10)`.
- Persists frozen quota, unlimited, and future fixed-period facts with deterministic 0-based `period_index`, period mode, formula metadata, weekday/frequency inputs, granted sessions, right/plan version identity, and contract snapshot. Zero-sized quota bags fail atomically with `bag_empty`.
- Materializes quota periods and matching period-scoped ledger grants in the caller's transaction. Stable idempotency keys, uniqueness constraints, locks, and request hashes prevent duplicates and reject conflicting replays. Unlimited stores one contract-wide period without a numeric bag or grant.
- Period request hashes bind the assignment and client identity plus plan/version, right, period coordinates, frozen calculation facts, and contract inputs. Replay recovery first scopes to exact assignment/client/right/index coordinates and then verifies the immutable period fields and expected key/hash; a key owned by another assignment is always a conflict. Grant replay verifies assignment, client, right, period, amount, request hash, and key. PostgreSQL constraints enforce duplicate/overlap/grant invariants; the materializer owns exact replay semantics around `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`.
- Fixed/pack/one-off activation is deliberately not connected: the F1-B materializer returns `payment_activation_required` for those terms until F1-C supplies the payment coordinator.
- The new grant invariant applies to F1-B quota periods. Legacy 0033 rows whose `entitlement_mode` is NULL retain the original grant behavior, including 098E payment activation.
- The current legacy sale guard remains intact: term-enabled plans are not assignable through the public route and still return `contract_terms_not_available`.
