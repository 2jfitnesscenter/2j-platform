## Approved functional rules checked

Source: `docs/architecture/epics/2J-098-master.md`, decisions P-02, P-05, P-08, P-12, P-13; contract table and payment model; monthly entitlement splitting examples.

- A quarterly contract from 15/10 through 14/01 is **one contract and one charge**, while entitlement is split into **four independent bags**: 15–31/10, 01–30/11, 01–31/12, and 01–14/01. The four bags are calendar slices, not four contracts or instalments. F1-C tests this distinction.
- For `custom_range` and `rolling_months`, the five complete natural days begin after the assignment date. Assignment 15/10 gives `due_on=20/10`; payment is valid through 23:59:59 Canary on that date. If blocking is enforced, it becomes effective at 00:00 Canary on 21/10.
- `due_on` remains the inclusive payment deadline. The next-local-midnight access-block date is recorded separately in the immutable deadline snapshot and is conditional on `payment_block='enforce'`.
- `natural_month` uses the service month's configured `due_day` (10), and only the first day or an earlier assignment is accepted. A natural month already in progress is rejected.
- An unpaid booking block affects new reservations for the relevant assignment; it does not cancel existing reservations or block account/profile/history access.

The previous merged schema did not yet contain the `economic_settings.grace_days` field referenced by the approved epic. Migration `0038_economic_grace_days.sql` adds it with the approved default of 5 and a bounded check; historical settings and assignments are not rewritten.

## Implemented locally

- `coordinateContractAssignment` is a server-side transaction service, not a public route. It checks permission, active client and catalog state, frozen current plan version, contract modality/date validity, and pricing authority.
- It calculates the authoritative `due_on`, then atomically writes exactly one charge, one assignment, immutable `assignment_contract_terms`, the initial assignment event, F1-B periods and grants, and audit records.
- The same idempotency key and canonical payload replay the original assignment. A different payload conflicts. PostgreSQL advisory locking serializes concurrent same-key calls. A failure in any initial period rolls the entire operation back.
- Legacy 098E sale behavior remains on its existing route. New contract plans remain unavailable from `/api/plans/assignable` and the existing public assignment route still responds with `contract_terms_not_available`.
- Pack/one-off activation, payment receipt, access-gate enforcement, F1-D, and F1-E remain outside this first F1-C implementation.
